WildWise · Master Roadmap

From one raccoon to a nationwide movement.

Everything starts with keeping Noodle safe. Everything else follows from there.

Phase 1 — Urgent

Protect Noodle

Do this week
1

Get Dave's veterinary letter signed

Official documentation of imprinting status, vaccination record, and non-releasable designation on practice letterhead.

2

Send the Blandford email

Reach out about an educational partnership. Lead with the story, not the legal situation. You already have a draft ready to go.

3

Contact other licensed rehabbers

Ask about working under their permit. Be transparent, lead with Noodle's story and your track record. Bring the vet letter.

4

Consider a wildlife attorney consult

One hour, one phone call. You're a customer buying information — not a defendant. It changes your position entirely.

Phase 2 — Brand

Build WildWise

Next 30–60 days
1

Launch the WildWise website

Homepage and About page are already designed. Add Noodle's story, educational content, and a contact form.

2

Finalize logo 4 and brand assets

Lock in the color palette, typography, and logo. Build out social media profiles under the WildWise brand.

3

Start telling Noodle's story publicly

Social content, short videos, Noodle being Noodle. An imprinted raccoon with a mission is an internet magnet. Use it.

4

Formalize as a 501(c)(3)

Nonprofit status unlocks grants, credibility, and the institutional affiliations needed for educational permits.

Phase 3 — Programs

Build the education layer

Months 2–6
1

Develop school ambassador curriculum

Noodle visits classrooms. Michigan wildlife, what to do when you find an animal, why rehabbers matter. Age-appropriate and compelling.

2

Create Wildlife First Responder training

The program that should have always existed. Community members trained in assessment, stabilization, and transfer. Certifiable and scalable.

3

Launch community programs

Libraries, community centers, nature centers, 4H chapters. Anywhere people gather and animals get found.

4

Build the transporter volunteer network

Recruit, lightly train, and map volunteer transporters across Michigan. Define service areas. Build the coordination framework.
