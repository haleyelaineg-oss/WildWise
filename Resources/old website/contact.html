Not a wildlife rescue?

WildWise is an education organization, not a wildlife rescue. If you have an animal that needs immediate help, please contact:

  • Michigan DNR Wildlife Division
  • A local licensed wildlife rehabilitator
  • Your county's animal control
  • Michigan Wildlife Hotline

Response times

We typically respond to all inquiries within 2–3 business days. School visit and partnership requests may take a bit longer during busy seasons.

Other ways to help

Every person who knows better
helps more wildlife.

You don't have to volunteer to make a difference. Sometimes the biggest impact is sharing what you know.

📢

Share the education

Every spring, someone in your life is going to find a "baby animal in need." Be the person who knows what to say. Share our education pages. Tag us.

🏫

Suggest a school visit

Know a teacher, librarian, or camp director? Suggest a Noodle visit. These programs reach kids at the age when wildlife habits form for life.

🤝

Connect us with organizations

We're looking to partner with parks, nature centers, conservation orgs, and vet clinics. If you know someone who should know us, make the introduction.

🚗

Join the transporter network

We're building a volunteer driver network to move animals from encounter to rehabber care faster. Have a car and a big heart? We want to hear from you.

📸

Follow and share our content

The more people who see our content, the more animals that don't get "rescued" unnecessarily. Follow us, engage, share. It matters.

💬

Tell us what your community needs

What wildlife questions come up in your neighborhood? What species? What mistakes? The more we hear, the more useful we can be. Drop us a line.
