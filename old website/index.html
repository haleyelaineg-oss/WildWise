The gap we aim to help close

The rehabber capacity crisis.

Michigan's licensed wildlife rehabilitators are overwhelmed. Every spring and summer, they're flooded with animal intakes. Many of these animals are healthy and don't need human help; they're just waiting for mom to come back. Well-meaning people often mistake them for being in danger and intervene, which creates a huge strain on rehabbers.

Every unnecessary intake takes capacity from an animal that genuinely needs help. Many rehabbers have to turn away animals in need because they're at capacity. That's the problem that inspired us to start WildWise. We want to help people understand when to help, when to wait, and who to call so that animals in need can get the care they deserve and healthy animals can stay wild.
